\chapter*{Acknowledgments}
%=========================================================

    First and foremost, I thank Elke Scheer for giving me the opportunity to conduct this research under her supervision. Her scientific guidance, the many discussions we had, and the freedom and resources she provided shaped this project throughout. Her support in the laboratory and her encouragement to present my work at conferences allowed me to engage deeply with mesoscopic superconductivity.
    
    While writing the theory part of this thesis, I came to appreciate how the different parts of the field come together in this project: microscopic quasiparticle tunneling, macroscopic Josephson dynamics, mesoscopic transport through atomic contacts, and stochastic energy exchange with the electromagnetic environment. All these perspectives ultimately meet in microwave driven superconducting devices, such as atomic contacts and single-electron transistors. I am very grateful that this project allowed me to work on a system at the heart of mesoscopic superconductivity.

    I thank Carlos Cuevas for his theoretical insight. He helped me interpret the measurements carefully and avoid overinterpretation. The numerical codes he provided allowed me to explore and compare the models considered in this thesis.

    I thank Patrick Raif, whose practical project and master's thesis I had the pleasure of supervising over almost two years. Working out experimental plans and solutions together sharpened my own thinking, and I learned a great deal from our exchanges. His careful sample fabrication and data analysis supported several of the atomic contact measurements I present in this thesis, and his diligence made supervising him particularly rewarding.

    I thank my longtime friend Florian Kayatz, whose early development of the instrument control framework I supervised. He structured and documented that code with great care and a strong emphasis on reproducibility, providing a foundation of high quality that I subsequently extended into the measurement software used throughout this thesis. I also thank David Capalbo and Lazkin Habo for their motivated engagement and their willingness to help out in the lab and with simulations.

    The weekly discussions at the ``Meso Supra'' meetings contributed greatly to my scientific development. I thank all participants for our discussions about mesoscopic superconductivity and laboratory work. I particularly appreciated the contributions of Laura Sobral Rey and Lukas Kammermeier concerning SSETs. I thank Roman Hartmann for his help with many practical challenges in the laboratory, especially those involving cryogenic equipment. I also thank Annika Zuschlag for her support with sample fabrication.

    I thank Ansgar Fischer and Ingmar Jäger from the scientific workshop for their expertise, patience, and careful realization of many technical modifications. I also thank Sabine Wallrabe for her administrative support and willingness to help.
