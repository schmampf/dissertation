\chapter*{Acknowledgments}

    First and foremost, I thank Elke Scheer for giving me the opportunity to conduct this research under her supervision. Her scientific guidance, the many discussions she shared with me, and the freedom and resources she provided shaped this project throughout. Her support in the laboratory and her encouragement to present my work at conferences allowed me to engage deeply with mesoscopic superconductivity. 
    
    While writing the theory part of this thesis, I realized more and more how the different parts of the field come together in this project: microscopic quasiparticle tunneling, macroscopic Josephson dynamics, mesoscopic transport through atomic contacts, and stochastic energy exchange with the electromagnetic environment. In the end, all these perspectives meet in microwave-driven superconducting devices, such as atomic contacts single-electron transistor. I am very grateful that this project allowed me to work on a system at the heart of mesoscopic superconductivity.

    I thank Carlos Cuevas for his theoretical insight. He helped me interpret the measurements carefully and avoid overinterpretation. The numerical codes he provided allowed me to explore and compare the models considered in this thesis.

    I thank Patrick Raif for almost two years of close collaboration during his practical- and master's project. I learned a great deal during this time, while Patrick and I developed experimental plans and solutions. His skill in sample fabrication and data analysis contributed substantially to the atomic-contact measurements. Similar standards and complementary ways of working made this collaboration particularly productive.
