    This chapter presents the experimental results in three blocks that mirror the
    transport hierarchy introduced in Chapter~\ref{ch:superconducting-transport}.
    The first block establishes a high-quality reference dataset on a tunnel barrier
    under microwave irradiation. The second block addresses atomic contacts and
    finite-transmission transport. The third block focuses on single-electron
    physics in gated devices.
