    The device investigated here it TB2 (OI-24d-09). Due to problems with collapsing resist masks during shadow evaporation, the finger was displaced slightly in order to reduce free standing length of the resist. The Setup as discribed in Patrick Raif is used for this measurements. The main difference is the filtering in the lines. We are not using the commercial thermalizer and filter, instead we use a train of copper powder filters and steel capillarry cables. The reference resistor is moved already to cold temperatures in oposition of martin prestel. The antenna is again used for these measurements.

    Disclaimer: I found an asymmetry with great Rectification during amplitude sweeps for frequencies between 7.78 and 8.05 GHz. This is a lot of data and hard to break down. So in the end of this section, i will show sequences of false color maps and sequences of curves at fixed amplitudes. 
    
    A great start to get a visual impression of the phenomen is to check figures fig:tb:asym:irradiation-iv to fig:tb:asym:irradiation-dvdi-img. Here we can see in the beginning and end of the sequence a normal proper behaviour, as we would expect it for BCS / PAT at rather low frequencies, as discussed in the previous section.

    We can observe the replicas left and right of the 2 Delta peaks in the differential conductance. However, for larger amplitudes, the distance between the replicas of the peak start to close again. This is due to high electronic temperatures of the junction.
