\section{Tunnel Barrier}
\label{sec:tunnelbarrier}
%=========================================================

    SIS tunnel spectroscopy provides indirect access to the quasiparticle density of states. Under microwave irradiation, photon assisted tunneling produces features displaced from the original spectral structure by integer multiples of the photon energy. Tunnel junctions therefore provide a controlled platform for testing the SIS transport model and the limits of the Tien-Gordon description.

    In this section, I investigate static and microwave driven quasiparticle transport in two similar aluminum tunnel junctions, denoted TB1 and TB2. I measured the devices with different versions of the setup, and their fabrication and local circuit environments were not identical. Their comparison therefore tests which features follow the common tunnel junction physics and which depend on the measurement conditions or electromagnetic environment.

    I first use the higher resolution measurements of TB1 to establish the static junction parameters, calibrate the effective temperature and microwave amplitude, and compare the irradiated response with the Tien-Gordon Model. I then turn to TB2 and characterize the bias asymmetric response that appears under microwave irradiation. This progression establishes the conventional reference behavior before examining the regime in which the minimal model is no longer sufficient.
