    The samples gap and critical temperature are 
    \begin{align}
        G_\mathrm{N} = 0.0138(1)\,G_0\,, &&
        \Delta_0 = 188.5(5)\,\mathrm{ueV}\,,\\
        T_\mathrm{c} = 1.21(1)\,\mathrm{K}\,, &&
        \mu_0H_\mathrm{c} = 28(1)\,\mathrm{mT}\,.
    \end{align}
    Critical temperature infered by Delta is $1.24$, whats close enough.
    The gap could be estimated by the fits in \ref{subsec:ac:pincode}, and their clustering value. Measurements in the tunnel limit, that could be fitted with the bcs theory confirm this gap. The critcal temperature is retrieved by a temperature study on the same contact in the tunnel limit.

    The sample in the unbroken state was characterized with Patrick and is described in \cite{raif_electronic_2024}. The order parameter in the unbroken state were found to be
    \begin{align}
        G_\mathrm{N} \geq 550\,G_0\,, &&
        I_\mathrm{sw} = 1.075(5)\,\mathrm{mA}\,, \\
        I_\mathrm{rt} = 0.595(5)\,\mathrm{mA}\,, &&
        \mu_0H_\mathrm{c} = 9.9(1)\,\mathrm{mT}\,.
    \end{align}
    Critical- / Switching current and normal state conductance strongly depend the state of the break junction.
